Good morning, respected teachers and my dear friends.

Today, I would like to speak about saving the environment. Our environment gives us air, water, food, and shelter. Unfortunately, pollution, deforestation, and plastic waste are harming our planet. We must understand that protecting nature is everyone's responsibility. We can start with small steps, such as planting trees, saving water, avoiding plastic bags, and keeping our surroundings clean. We should also encourage others to follow these habits. Remember, we do not inherit the Earth only for ourselves; we must protect it for future generations. Let us work together to make our planet greener and cleaner.

Thank you!
